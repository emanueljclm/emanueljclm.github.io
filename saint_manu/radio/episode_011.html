A dark era — graduation followed by months of unemployment, moving back home, downsizing. The summer that was supposed to be New York for grad school became a necessary gap year. This playlist was built as a coping mechanism: an imaginary queer nightlife scene in Brooklyn during June, the music you'd hear if the night could be exactly what you needed it to be.
