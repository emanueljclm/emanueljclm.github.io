The genesis. A sophomore year marked by grief from the loss of an older sister, and the disorienting first love of a queer young adult. Saint Manu Radio began as a way out of creative block — picking up a guitar, exploring new sounds, building a playlist wide enough to hold all of it. R&B, hip-hop, dance, pop, and the experimental edges between them.
